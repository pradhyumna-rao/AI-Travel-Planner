# AI Travel Planner

## Overview

AI Travel Planner is an intelligent travel planning platform designed to help users organise and manage their entire 
holiday experience from start to finish. The system leverages AI and real-time travel data to research destinations, 
compare flight and accommodation options, and generate personalised travel itineraries tailored to each user's preferences.
By analysing factors such as budget, travel dates, interests, weather conditions, and local attractions, the platform 
aims to simplify the planning process while helping users save both time and money. Users can review, customise, and 
update their itineraries at any stage, ensuring complete flexibility and control over their travel plans.

---

## Key Features

* Flight search and price comparison across multiple providers
* Hotel and accommodation recommendations
* Personalised day-by-day itinerary generation
* Attraction and activity recommendations
* Budget tracking and cost estimation
* Travel time and route optimisation
* Weather-aware travel planning
* Interactive itinerary updates and travel advice
* Safety-focused accommodation recommendations
* Continuous itinerary refinement based on user feedback

## Project Goal

The goal of this project is to develop an intelligent travel assistant that automates the research, 
comparison, and planning process involved in organising a trip. By combining AI-driven recommendations with real-time 
travel information, the platform aims to provide users with personalised, efficient, and cost-effective travel plans 
that align with their individual preferences and requirements.

## Example Use Case: Iceland Winter Ring Road Trip

### Scenario Overview

A family of four from Ireland is planning a week-long winter trip to Iceland, titled **“The Iceland Winter Run”**. They will arrive in Reykjavík on the **21st of December** and plan to complete a full **Ring Road (Route 1)** road trip around the island.

Their primary preference is to travel in a **camper van**, allowing them to stay flexible and experience Iceland’s natural landscapes up close. A standard rental car with accommodation stops is also acceptable if weather conditions require adjustments.

They are particularly excited about visiting the **Blue Lagoon**, along with other iconic natural attractions. While they intend to follow the Ring Road, they are open to short detours to visit major landmarks and scenic highlights.

---

## Example Itinerary (7 Days)

### Day 1 — Arrival in Reykjavík (21st December)

* Arrive at Keflavík International Airport
* Pick up camper van rental
* Stock up on groceries and winter supplies in Reykjavík
* Explore Reykjavík city (Hallgrímskirkja Church, Sun Voyager, Harpa Concert Hall)
* Overnight stay in Reykjavík or nearby campsite

---

### Day 2 — Golden Circle Route

* Þingvellir National Park (tectonic plates & historical site)
* Geysir geothermal area (Strokkur eruption)
* Gullfoss waterfall
* Optional stop: Kerið volcanic crater
* Overnight camping near Selfoss or Hella

---

### Day 3 — South Coast Waterfalls & Black Sand Beaches

* Seljalandsfoss waterfall (walk behind the falls if safe)
* Skógafoss waterfall
* Reynisfjara black sand beach (basalt columns & Atlantic waves)
* Dyrhólaey viewpoint (weather permitting)
* Overnight near Vík

---

### Day 4 — Vatnajökull National Park

* Skaftafell Nature Reserve hiking trails
* Svartifoss waterfall
* Glacier viewpoints
* Optional glacier activity (guided tour)
* Overnight near Höfn

---

### Day 5 — East Fjords Drive

* Scenic coastal drive through East Fjords
* Stop at small fishing villages (Djúpivogur, Seyðisfjörður detour optional)
* Wildlife spotting (reindeer, coastal birds)
* Overnight in Egilsstaðir region

---

### Day 6 — North Iceland Highlights

* Lake Mývatn geothermal area
* Námaskarð hot springs
* Goðafoss waterfall
* Optional stop in Akureyri (Northern capital)
* Overnight stay in North Iceland campsite or guest area

---

### Day 7 — Return to Reykjavík via West Iceland

* Long drive back to Reykjavík
* Optional stops: Hraunfossar waterfalls, Deildartunguhver hot spring
* Final evening in Reykjavík
* Prepare for departure

---

### Day 8 — Blue Lagoon & Departure

* Morning visit to the **Blue Lagoon**
* Relax in geothermal spa before flight
* Return camper van
* Depart from Keflavík International Airport

---

## Notes

* Winter driving conditions may affect travel times and route safety
* Flexible routing is recommended due to weather variability
* Campsites may have limited winter availability, so backup accommodation options are advised
* Northern Lights sightings are possible throughout the trip depending on conditions
